parser: month and day swapped

The shared parser reads every date month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first, so 4 March comes out as 3 April. It reaches us by app, email and form, from every tenant. This is the fault #4471 names. Fixed by fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales; its ticket is closed.

hint: the booking-v3 form asks for the month first

booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. A day-first tenant’s clients type the month first, as the hint tells them, and the form then reads what they typed day first. The result is a swapped month and day, the same as the parser fault, so nothing in the body tells them apart. A parser fix does not change the hint. Won’t fix: the booking-v3 form is retired, so no new bookings come through it. Its ticket is closed.

The desk can only see it in reports that came in through the form of a tenant on booking-v3. A booking made on that form but reported by app or email counts under parser, so this count is a floor.

shift: a day off, not a swap

These reports do not swap month and day. Each is off by a day or by a time zone: 12 May shows as 11 May, a client in Lisbon gets Tuesday while a client in Toronto gets Monday, an invite arrives before the booking was made. The parser fix changes none of them. The first suspect is a time zone conversion; the ticket asks engineering to confirm it. Open: no fix is planned yet. The desk listed them in desk-notes.json as one-day moves and then counted them under parser.

How to tell them apart

  1. Ignore the subject line. The same subjects (“Date is wrong”, “Month and day swapped?”) come in for every problem here.
  2. Ignore intake’s tag. Intake guesses from the reporter’s browser; the cause depends on the tenant. The table counts how many tags are wrong.
  3. Read the body. A date off by a day or a time zone, with month and day not swapped, is shift: add its id to the one-day moves in desk-notes.json.
  4. Otherwise, a report that came in through a booking-v3 form is hint. Anything else is parser.

Why one ticket hid the rest

Until 2 July nothing was merged. That day the desk merged every report received since 1 April into #4471 at once, across all subjects, and kept merging new ones as they came. A merged ticket is counted as one bug. Merge a report into a ticket only when it shares the ticket’s cause, not just its symptom.
